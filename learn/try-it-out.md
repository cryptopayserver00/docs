On this page you will see various ways to test out the software on mainnet and testnet.

# **Our Mainnet demo server**

We host our server on [cryptopayserver.online](https://cryptopayserver.online/)

Feel free to register to it, create a store and a wallet and play around.

# **Our Testing demo server**

If you're familiar with testing and want to test CryptoPay through it, check [cryptopayserver.online](https://cryptopayserver.online/).

# **Third-party hosts**

**Third-party hosts** provide hosted solutions, in most cases free of charge, in case you don't want to self-host a server.

While most of them can be used in-production, you can also use them to try things out.

They're similar to our demo server, but provide better uptime and reliability.

For full lists of third-party hosts to pick from, **see our hosts directory**.

# **Start testing**

Once you pick up a server to use, the steps are the same, regardless of which option you've opted for.

Click on the following links for detailed instructions on how to:
