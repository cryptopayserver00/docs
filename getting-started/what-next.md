After you've connected your wallet to your CryptoPay, you can explore various built in tools in the software. See the full list of Use Cases **here**.

# Creating the Point of Sale

CryptoPay has a PoS which you can use to receive payments from your customers directly or to receive donations. To **create the POS**, you need to have a store created in CryptoPay. View PoS instructions **here**.

# Creating the Crowdfund

Create a **crowdfunding campaign using CryptoPay**. Unlike traditional crowdfunding platforms, the creator of the campaign is the owner of the platform. Funds go directly to the creator's wallet without any fees. View Crowdfund instructions **here**.

# Creating the Payment Request

Create custom invoices which can be shared with others, by sending a link to your payment request. Users can pay the request at any time. CryptoPay automatically updates the Crypto exchange rate at the time of payment. View Payment Request instructions **here**.

# Creating the Pay Button

**Pay buttons** are great when you have a fixed amount for a product or a donation. You can easily embed the button into HTML. When the customer or a visitor clicks on the button, CryptoPay displays a checkout page and an invoice for them. View Pay Button instructions **here**.

# Connecting your CryptoPay store to your e-commerce platform

Depending on the CMS you're using, you can easily connect CryptoPay to your online store. Currently, CryptoPay offers following integrations:

* Shopify
* Wordpress
* WooCommerce
* Custom integration

# Connecting Additional CryptoPay Services

Additional free, open-source plugins can be used with your CryptoPay.

* LibrePatron
* Others

**LibrePatron is a self-hosted, censorship-free, Patreon alternative**. LibrePatron uses CryptoPay Server to receive Bitcoin payments on a recurring basis. Funds go directly to the creator's wallet. View LibrePatron instructions **here**.

# Join The CryptoPay Community

CryptoPay Server is an open-source project, not a company. We rely on a network of diverse contributors and users to provide support for numerous use-cases. Join us in improving, learning and building CryptoPay.

If you have questions, try searching out FAQ Section or join the CryptoPay Community and share questions and ideas for improvement.
