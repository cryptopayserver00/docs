The primary purpose of CryptoPay Server is to remove dependencies on trusted third-parties. The Plugins are built in applications that obsolete central-authorities and allow users an easy way to extend the **use case** of the software. Users can self-host all sorts of customizable applications that work out of the box.

To create an plugin, go to Plugins > Create a new plugin. Plugins are store-dependent, which means that each plugin needs to be connected to a store.

# Point of Sale

The **web-based PoS** allows users with brick and mortar stores to readily **accept cryptocurrencies without fees or third-party**, directly to their wallet. The **PoS** can be displayed easily on tablets or any other devices which support web browsing. Users can easily create a homescreen shortcut for a quick access to the web-plugin.

Adding new products is easy. The plugin has a **shopping cart feature, tips, product inventory, custom payment options** and more.

The **Point of sale** can also be used to receive donations, tips or even as a small e-commerce shop, depending on the options or customizations applied.

Currently, the **Point of Sale** supports there different views:

- A `Static` view representing only the items of sale.
- A `Cart` view including items for sale and a cart for checkout.
- A `Light` view consisting only of a keypad for easy and quick payments.

To get your first **Point of Sale** running, follow theses few simple steps:

1. Go to `Plugins` and `Create a new plugin`
2. Add a `name` for your plugin
3. Choose `plugin type` > Point Of Sale
4. Select the `store` to associate with the plugin.
5. Customize your PoS by choosing a `view` (Static, Cart, Light), adding your own `items` with prices, photos, and a description.
6. Click `Save Settings.`
7. Click `View Plugin` to view your PoS (your customers can access the PoS through that link).

You can change the appearance of your **Point of Sale** by following the **theme customization guide**.

# Payment Button

Easily-embeddable HTML and highly-customizable **payment buttons** allow users to receive tips and donations. Online stores can also integrate payment buttons. When a site visitor clicks on the button, CryptoPay displays the **invoice**.

1. In your left menu bar, under the "PLUGINS" section, select "Pay Button".
2. Allow anyone to create invoices.
3. Customize your button.
4. Copy the generated from and embed it on your website.

# Crowdfunding

**Crowdfunding** is an application which you can launch from CryptoPay Server interface that allows you to create a **self-hosted funding campaign**, similar to Kickstarter or Indiegogo. Unlike traditional **crowdfunding platforms**, the creator of the campaign is the owner of the platform. Funds go directly to the creator's wallet **without any fees**.

1. Go to > Plugins
2. Add a name of your plugin
3. Choose plugin type > Crowdfund
4. Select the store to associate with the plugin.
5. Customize your Crowdfund by adding your own perks with prices, photos, and description.
6. Check the box > Allow crowdfund to be publicly visible
7. Click "Save Settings".
8. Click "View Plugin" to view your Crowdfund (Contributors can access the crowdfund through that link).

If you would like to provide digital or physical products to the backers of your crowdfunding campaign, you can integrate WooCommerce store into it. You can also set limits on contribution perks using the inventory feature.
